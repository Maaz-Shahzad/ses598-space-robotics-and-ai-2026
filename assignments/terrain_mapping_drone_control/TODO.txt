Option 1 — rosbag record + offline rtabmap processing
Step 1
 — Delete the old database
bash: rm ~/.ros/rtabmap.db

Step 2
 — Launch the world and mission as normal

Terminal 1:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
ros2 launch terrain_mapping_drone_control rover_world.launch.py

Terminal 2:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
ros2 launch terrain_mapping_drone_control rover_mission.launch.py

Terminal 3 — start the odom converter:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
python3 ~/ros2_ws/src/terrain_mapping_drone_control/terrain_mapping_drone_control/px4_odom_converter.py

Step 3 — Wait for the drone to take off, then start recording

Terminal 4 — once the drone is airborne:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
ros2 bag record \
  /drone/front_rgb \
  /drone/front_depth \
  /drone/front_rgb/camera_info \
  /drone/front_depth/camera_info \
  /drone/odom \
  /tf \
  /tf_static \
  -o ~/ros2_ws/mission_bag


Step 4 — Let the drone complete its full mission
Wait until the drone finishes the spiral and lands. Then stop the recording with Ctrl+C in Terminal 4. Check the bag was recorded:
bash: ros2 bag info ~/ros2_ws/mission_bag
You should see all 7 topics with message counts.

Step 5 — Stop everything except the odom converter
Stop Terminal 1 (world), Terminal 2 (mission). Keep Terminal 3 (converter) running or stop it — it's not needed for replay.

Step 6 — Delete old database and launch rtabmap for offline processing
bash: rm ~/.ros/rtabmap.db

Terminal 1 — launch rtabmap:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
ros2 launch terrain_mapping_drone_control rtabmap_jazzy_droneOdo.launch.py

Terminal 2 — replay the bag at full speed:
bash: source ~/ros2_ws/rosenv.sh
ros2 bag play ~/ros2_ws/mission_bag --clock --rate 1.0
Watch the rtabmap terminal — the delay should stay near 0 since it's processing at the same rate as the bag plays. Wait for the bag to finish playing completely.

Step 7 — Stop rtabmap cleanly and export
bash: # Ctrl+C in rtabmap terminal, then:

cd ~/.ros
rtabmap-export \
  --cloud \
  --voxel 0.02 \
  --min_cluster_size 50 \
  --poses_camera \
  --output mission_map \
  rtabmap.db
ls ~/.ros/mission_map_cloud.ply

Step 8 — View the result
bash: sudo apt install cloudcompare -y
cloudcompare ~/.ros/mission_map_cloud.ply

The key advantage over what you had before is that the bag replay is controlled — no real-time pressure, no delay buildup, and you can replay it as many times as you want with different rtabmap settings without re-running the simulation.
